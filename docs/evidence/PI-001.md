# PI-001…PI-008 — Project intelligence and outcome quality

Built 2026-11-14. Design and gap analysis: [`docs/INTELLIGENCE-PLATFORM.md`](../INTELLIGENCE-PLATFORM.md).

## Why

A full review of the repository against the product brief found two gaps that mattered most
to a developer after an AI session:

1. There was no way to ask the project, or its AI history, a question. D-008 had dropped a
   retrieval index because Cursor's codebase search serves the agent; nothing served "what did
   Cursor change last session, and why?" (`fts_docs` existed in migration 1 but was never written).
2. Reviews judged process (reads, retries, delegation), not outcome. No code computed the facts
   an outcome judgement needs, so a review could not cite them.
